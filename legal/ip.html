LEXDEN NOVA — INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, COPYRIGHT & TAKEDOWN POLICY

Version: 1.0 Effective date: 2026-10-25 Legal notices: the contact form on this website

1. Creator responsibility

Every Creator must own, control or have adequate legal permission to publish and sell material uploaded to NOVA, including text, images, music, video, software, course materials, datasets, logos and testimonials.

2. Copyright complaints

A rights holder may send a written notice claiming infringement. The notice should identify:

  • the protected work;
  • the allegedly infringing content and enough information to locate it;
  • the complainant's contact information;
  • a statement explaining the good-faith basis of the claim;
  • a statement that the information is accurate and the complainant is authorized to act; and
  • a physical/electronic signature where required.

NOVA may request additional information needed to assess the claim.

3. Response

NOVA may remove or disable access to allegedly infringing material where required by law, provider rules or a substantiated platform decision.

4. Counter-notice / response from Creator

Where a counter-notice or response process is legally or operationally appropriate, NOVA may request evidence of authorization and may notify/forward relevant information to the complaining party where lawful and necessary.

5. Repeat infringement

NOVA may suspend or terminate repeat infringers where appropriate.

6. Other rights

Trademark, privacy, publicity, confidential-information and other rights complaints may also be reported.

7. Bad-faith complaints

False or knowingly abusive complaints may be rejected and can affect the complainant's account where appropriate.

8. Legal basis

The Nigerian Copyright Act 2022 contains specific online-content provisions concerning notice to take down or disable access to allegedly infringing content. NOVA's procedure is designed to accommodate those statutory requirements without implying that every claim is automatically proven on receipt.

LEXDEN NOVA CREATOR — SUSPENSION, ENFORCEMENT & APPEALS POLICY

Version: 1.0 Effective date: 2026-10-25

1. Risk-based enforcement

NOVA uses proportionate enforcement. A weak automated signal should not automatically be described as proof of criminal conduct.

2. Typical actions

  • change requested;
  • warning;
  • listing pause;
  • restricted visibility;
  • Creator REVIEW/RESTRICTED status;
  • payout hold;
  • suspension;
  • termination/permanent exclusion.

3. Immediate action

NOVA may act immediately for suspected malware, significant fraud, serious consumer safety risk, unlawful products, account takeover, serious payment abuse or other circumstances where delay creates material risk.

4. Payout controls

A suspension can also affect the timing of payouts where funds are subject to refunds, chargebacks, disputes, reserve requirements or legal/provider restrictions.

5. Appeal

Where an appeal is offered, provide the decision reference, relevant facts, evidence and requested remedy. NOVA may preserve confidential fraud-detection methods where disclosure would weaken safety controls.

6. Reinstatement

Reinstatement is not automatic. NOVA may require corrected information, evidence of authorization, remediation or additional verification.

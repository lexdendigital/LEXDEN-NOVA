LEXDEN NOVA CREATOR — CREATOR TERMS & SELLER AGREEMENT

Version: 1.0-draft Effective date: 2026-10-22 Operator: LEXDEN NOVA (an unregistered start-up run by its founder), operating LEXDEN NOVA

1. Scope

These Creator Terms apply to every Personal Creator or Business Creator approved to offer goods/services through LEXDEN NOVA Creator.

2. Eligibility

Creator selling and payout participation is 18+ by default unless NOVA separately launches a guardian-managed programme with a verified legal/payment basis.

Creators must provide accurate information and complete verification required for the feature. NOVA may request identity, business, address, contact and payout information.

3. Approval does not mean endorsement

Approval means that NOVA has approved the Creator/application for participation at that time. It does not guarantee future compliance, product quality, or fraud-free activity.

4. Product responsibility

Creators are responsible for the legality, truthfulness, quality, fulfilment, customer support, intellectual-property rights, supplier permissions and product-specific disclosures applicable to their offerings.

5. Product types

NOVA may support physical products, digital downloads, eBooks, templates, software, courses, external-access products, services, live programmes and other approved categories.

6. Product approval and versioning

Products may require administrative approval before publication. Approved products should use immutable published versions. A Creator edit may create a new version for review rather than silently changing the historical approved product.

7. Creator policies

A Creator may configure product-specific refund, delivery, warranty, access and support rules, but those rules must not conflict with NOVA mandatory policies or applicable law.

8. Customer support

Creators must maintain the support contact shown on their listing and respond within the operational standards presented by NOVA.

9. Physical fulfilment

Creators must accurately define dispatch times, stock/supplier arrangements, delivery coverage and tracking. Do not claim broader coverage than the approved fulfilment setup supports.

10. Digital products

Creators must own or be authorized to distribute paid files. Pirated libraries, cracked software, credential stealers, malware and other unauthorized content are prohibited.

11. External-access products

Creators must provide a functional access method, support route, expected activation time and, where practical, a fallback method.

12. Services and programmes

Creators must define scope, deliverables, turnaround, revisions, required buyer information and completion criteria.

13. Intellectual property

The Creator retains ownership of Creator content subject to the limited licence stated in the Creator IP Policy. The Creator warrants that the content may lawfully be provided to NOVA for marketplace operation.

14. Supplier integrations

Where a creator-owned supplier connection is required, the Creator must use authorized accounts and protect credentials. Supplier credentials must never be exposed to buyers or stored in public product fields.

15. Fees

NOVA may charge platform, transaction, withdrawal, affiliate or other fees. The applicable fee must be disclosed before the relevant commitment. Rates may change prospectively for legitimate commercial, provider, tax, operational or risk reasons, subject to applicable notice requirements.

16. Payouts

A Creator's ledger balance is not necessarily withdrawable immediately. NOVA may apply pending, reserve, dispute, chargeback, refund or compliance controls.

NOVA may hold, delay, reverse or offset amounts where reasonably necessary for refunds, chargebacks, fraud investigation, legal process or reconciliation, subject to applicable law.

17. Anti-circumvention

Do not redirect a transaction initiated on NOVA to an unapproved external payment route to avoid platform fees, buyer protections, records, review or risk controls.

18. Reviews

Creators must not buy, fabricate, manipulate or suppress reviews, or disclose private buyer data in response to reviews.

19. Records

Creators should keep records reasonably necessary to demonstrate ownership, fulfilment, customer support, refunds and compliance.

20. Taxes and licenses

Creators are responsible for taxes, licences, permits, registrations, employment obligations and other legal requirements applicable to their business/activity.

21. Enforcement

NOVA may use automated checks, risk signals and human review. Outcomes can include changes requested, rejection, pause, restricted visibility, suspension, payout hold, termination or permanent exclusion.

22. Termination

A Creator may close the account subject to outstanding orders, refunds, disputes, payouts and legal/financial record obligations. NOVA may suspend or terminate an account when permitted by law and policy.

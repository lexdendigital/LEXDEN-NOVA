LEXDEN NOVA — PRIVACY NOTICE

Version: 1.0 Effective date: 2026-10-25 Operator: LEXDEN NOVA (an unregistered start-up run by its founder), trading as/operating LEXDEN NOVA Contact: lexdendigital@gmail.com Privacy/DPO contact: lexdendigital@gmail.com for now

1. Who this notice is for

This Notice applies to visitors, buyers, Creators, Affiliates, support users and other people who interact with LEXDEN NOVA.

LEXDEN is a wider enterprise/group. Different LEXDEN products (for example LEXDEN Academy, LEXDEN Studio and other future products) may have separate processing activities. Where another LEXDEN service has a separate notice, that service's notice should also be reviewed.

2. What data we may process

Depending on how you use NOVA, we may process:

  • account data: name, email, phone number, username and account identifiers;
  • age/date-of-birth information where needed for eligibility, safety or verification;
  • public profile information supplied for creator pages;
  • business/creator verification information and document references;
  • transaction information: orders, products, prices, references, refunds, disputes and payout records;
  • delivery information: delivery address, service area, dispatch and tracking details;
  • digital entitlement/access records;
  • support tickets, messages, complaints, reviews and reports;
  • device, browser, IP-derived security signals, logs and similar technical information;
  • cookies, local-storage values and analytics information according to the Cookies Notice;
  • affiliate/referral attribution information;
  • AI interaction data where needed to provide the feature, protect security, comply with law or maintain auditability.

We do not intend to collect more personal data than is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

3. Why we use data

NOVA may use personal data to:

  • create and manage accounts;
  • display public seller/creator profiles;
  • provide discovery, marketplace, checkout, delivery and digital-access services;
  • verify Creator identity/business information;
  • process or reconcile transactions, refunds, disputes and payouts;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, malware, account takeover and payment circumvention;
  • protect the security of the platform and users;
  • communicate about orders, service messages and support matters;
  • send marketing communications where a lawful basis and required consent/control exists;
  • provide affiliate attribution and commission administration;
  • monitor product/access health and marketplace integrity;
  • support AI features and audit user-approved AI actions;
  • comply with law, lawful requests and provider rules;
  • improve reliability, usability and product safety.

4. Lawful bases

The applicable lawful basis depends on the processing activity. NOVA may rely on contract, legal obligation, legitimate interests or consent where appropriate under applicable law. Consent is not treated as the only possible lawful basis.

Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawfully completed before withdrawal, subject to legal limitations.

5. Children and younger users

NOVA is designed to provide access to useful resources, including free/public resources, while applying additional safeguards to higher-risk features.

Some features may be restricted to adults or require parental/guardian involvement because they involve payment, selling, payouts, contracts, verification or other material obligations.

Where the law requires parental/guardian consent, age assurance or additional safeguards for a child's personal data, NOVA will apply those requirements. Free access does not automatically make privacy rules disappear.

NOVA does not intentionally create a system allowing minors to bypass a legal age or consent requirement by falsely representing their age.

6. Public information

Some Creator profile information is intentionally public so buyers can understand who is offering a product/service. Private KYC records, bank details, private delivery information and similar confidential data are not intended for public display.

7. Sharing

Depending on the service, NOVA may share necessary information with:

  • payment providers such as Paystack;
  • cloud/hosting, database and security providers;
  • email and communications providers;
  • logistics/fulfilment providers;
  • file/media storage providers;
  • analytics or monitoring providers where enabled;
  • professional advisers or authorities where legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

Third-party providers have their own terms and privacy documents.

8. International transfers

NOVA may serve international buyers and may use technology/service providers located outside Nigeria. Personal data may therefore be processed or transferred outside Nigeria where permitted by applicable law and subject to the required safeguards.

NOVA will not treat international availability as a waiver of data-protection rights. Where another jurisdiction's data-protection law applies to a particular processing activity, NOVA will assess the additional obligations that may arise.

9. Data security

NOVA uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure. No internet system can be promised to be perfectly secure.

Sensitive data should not be stored in browser local storage merely for convenience. Access to KYC, payout and other sensitive information should be restricted by role and logged.

10. Retention

NOVA keeps data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legal/compliance requirements, dispute handling, fraud prevention, accounting/financial records, security and legitimate business needs.

The Internal Data Retention Schedule provides operational targets; a legal retention obligation overrides an operational deletion target where applicable.

11. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights including information, access, rectification, objection, restriction, portability, erasure/forgetting and rights relating to certain automated decisions. You may also have the right to complain to the supervisory authority.

For Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and current NDPC guidance are the principal references for these rights.

12. Automated decision-making and AI

NOVA may use automated systems for classification, risk signals, product checks, fraud indicators, access controls and other operational purposes.

A material decision should not be described as legally conclusive merely because an automated system generated it. Where applicable law gives a person rights concerning solely automated decisions with significant effects, NOVA will provide the safeguards required by that law.

13. Marketing communications

Transactional messages are separated from marketing preferences. Marketing communications should use a lawful basis and, where consent is required, a clear opt-in and an easy opt-out.

Unsubscribing from marketing does not necessarily stop essential transaction/security messages.

14. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

NOVA's use of cookies and similar technologies is explained in the Cookies & Similar Technologies Notice.

The current application uses browser storage for some preferences and activity. Sensitive information should not be unnecessarily placed in local storage.

15. Data breach response

NOVA maintains an internal breach-response process. Where applicable law requires notification to a regulator or affected people, NOVA will follow the relevant legal requirements and timelines.

LEXDEN NOVA CREATOR — VERIFICATION & KYC NOTICE

Version: 1.0-draft Effective date: 2026-10-22

1. Why verification is used

NOVA verifies Creator information to reduce impersonation, fraud, payment misuse, consumer harm and marketplace abuse and to satisfy provider/legal requirements where applicable.

2. Information that may be requested

Depending on Creator type and feature, NOVA may request:

  • legal/original name;
  • date of birth;
  • email and phone;
  • delivery area;
  • category/niche;
  • profile image;
  • social profiles;
  • business name;
  • customer-care email/WhatsApp;
  • business/address evidence such as appropriate registration or address documentation;
  • payout-account information and related verification data.

3. Verification badge

A badge means only that NOVA completed the relevant verification step. It is not a guarantee of product quality, solvency, honesty or future compliance.

4. Processing and access

KYC documents must be stored in a restricted environment, accessible only to authorized personnel/services, with appropriate logging and retention limits.

5. Verification outcomes

A Creator application may be:

SUBMITTED, AUTOMATED_CHECK, NEEDS_INFORMATION, HUMAN_REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED, SUSPENDED, BANNED.

6. Target review time

NOVA may communicate a target such as "most applications are targeted for review within 24 hours" rather than an absolute guarantee.

Complex, incomplete or higher-risk cases may take longer.

7. Fraud / misuse

Where an application is found to contain material deception or serious misuse, NOVA may restrict re-registration using protected contact or identity signals where lawful and proportionate. Such controls must not become a public blacklist.

8. Rights

KYC data is personal data and is handled under the Privacy Notice and applicable law.

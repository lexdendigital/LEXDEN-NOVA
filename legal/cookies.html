LEXDEN NOVA — COOKIES & SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES NOTICE

Version: 1.0-draft Effective date: 2026-10-22 Contact: lexdendigital@gmail.com

1. Scope

This Notice explains how LEXDEN NOVA may use cookies, browser storage, pixels, SDKs, tags and similar technologies.

2. Current categories

Strictly necessary

Authentication, security, session continuity, basic functionality and other features required to provide the Service.

Preferences

Theme, interface choices and similar user preferences.

Analytics

Reliability, performance and aggregate product-usage measurement where enabled and legally permitted.

Marketing / advertising

Only where such technologies are actually deployed and the required consent or other lawful basis exists.

3. Local storage

The NOVA application may use browser local storage for preferences, drafts, cached information and other client-side functionality. Local storage is not a safe substitute for protected server storage of passwords, secret keys, KYC documents, payment credentials or other highly sensitive information.

4. Third-party services

Third-party services may set or access their own identifiers according to their own technical designs. Depending on the feature, providers may include Firebase, Paystack, Cloudinary, Brevo, analytics/monitoring tools or future infrastructure providers.

The final published tracker list must be generated from the production build and provider configuration. This Notice must not claim that a tracking service exists if it is not actually deployed.

5. Consent

Where consent is legally required for non-essential cookies or tracking, NOVA should present a clear choice before activation. The preference mechanism should not use dark patterns or force a user to accept marketing tracking as a condition of accessing unrelated functions.

6. Changes

The Notice must be updated whenever the production tracking inventory materially changes.

LEXDEN NOVA Affiliate Programme: earn commission by sharing products

The Nova Affiliate Programme lets you earn a commission when someone buys a LEXDEN NOVA product through your personal link. You do not need to create a product, hold stock or deliver anything.

How it works

  1. Create your affiliate account.
  2. Copy your personal link for any product or for the whole store.
  3. Share it on WhatsApp, social media or anywhere your audience is.
  4. When a customer buys through your link, the commission is recorded in your dashboard.
  5. Request a withdrawal to your bank account when you reach the minimum.

Rates and payouts

  • The standard commission is 10% of the sale value. Individual products can set a different rate, shown in your affiliate dashboard.
  • The minimum withdrawal is ₦5,000.
  • A withdrawal fee of 2.5% applies when you cash out.

SELL YOUR FIRST DIGITAL PRODUCT IN 7 DAYS 🚀

Published 2026-10-14

You don't need a big idea. You need a small problem and one week. 👇

Swipe through the slides, then follow this simple plan:

📅 DAY 1 — Pick ONE problem
What do people always ask you for help with? CV writing? Budgeting? Using AI tools? Start there.

📅 DAY 2–4 — Build the simplest version
A short ebook, a checklist, or a template. Not perfect. Useful.

📅 DAY 5–6 — Set your price and make your link
Keep it affordable. Make it easy to say yes.

📅 DAY 7 — Share it
WhatsApp status, group chats, your bio. Tell people what it fixes for them.

The first sale is not about the money. It proves the whole thing is possible. ✅

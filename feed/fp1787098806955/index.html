CLAUDE + YOUTUBE SHORTS = A POWERFUL CONTENT ENGINE.

Published 2026-08-19

No camera.
No face.
No complicated editing.

Just Claude, the right prompts, and a repeatable workflow for creating Shorts faster.
I’m sharing the 12 exact prompts you can use to build, script, optimize, and scale your Shorts. 👇

Save this. You’ll want these later.

1. 90% fail by choosing the wrong niche.

Prompt:

"I am [AGE] years old and interested in [TOPICS].

Give me the 5 Shorts niches with the highest CPM.

Include: competition, actual revenue at 10K subs, and which one to start TODAY with no money.

Order by monetization speed."

2. A short without a script is a dead short.

Prompt:

"Write a 45-second short script about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].

Include:

Hook in the first 3 seconds

3 key points with facts

Final CTA that generates comments

Conversational tone, short sentences."

3. If you lose the viewer in 3 seconds, you lose everything.

Prompt:

"Give me 20 one-line hooks for a Short about

[TOPIC].

5 of curiosity. 5 of shock. 5 of direct question.

5 of brutal promise.

Each hook must have fewer than 10 words and sound like something a real human.

4. The title decides whether they open you or ignore you.

Prompt:

"Generate 15 titles for a Short about [TOPIC].

5 with numbers. 5 with questions. 5 with emotional tension.

Less than 60 characters.

Add which one has the most viral potential and why."

Y
5. You don't need your voice. Nor to appear on camera.

Prompt:

"Adapt this script for Al-generated voice-over (ElevenLabs or similar).

Mark pauses with [...], emphasis with UPPERCASE and emotion between parentheses.

Script:

[PASTE YOUR SCRIPT]"

6. The algorithm rewards those who stay until the end.

Prompt:

"Restructure this script for maximum retention in Shorts.

Add:

Loop at the beginning and end

Cliffhanger at 15s

Unexpected twist at 30s

Give me the second-by-second optimized version."

7. Your description is free SEO. Most people waste it.

Prompt:

"Create the perfect description for a Short on [TOPIC].

Include:

First line with exact keyword

3 hook lines

15 hashtags (5 big, 5 medium, 5 niche)"

8. The problem isn't making 1 short. It's making 100.

Prompt:

"Give me 30 short ideas about [NICHE] for the next 30 days.

Each idea with:

Title

1-line hook

Unique angle

Don't repeat concepts. Mix educational, viral, and controversial

9. 1 long video = 10 shorts.

Prompt:

Analyze this long script and extract 10 independent shorts.

Each with:

Own hook

Closed beginning and end

Between 30 and 60 seconds

Script: [PASTE YOUR CONTENT]''

10. AdSense is the beginning. Not the end.

Prompt:

"I'm a creator of Shorts about [NICHE] with [X] subscribers.

Give me 7 ways to monetize right now.

Order by: realistic first-month revenue + ease of implementation.

No theory. Concrete examples."

11. Don't invent the wheel. Copy what already works.

Prompt:

"Analyze these 5 Shorts channels about

[NICHE]: [NAMES].

Give me:

Common pattern in their hooks

Structure of their titles

What the ones growing fastest do differently

3 gaps I can exploit"

12. Posting whenever you feel like it = algorithm against you.

Prompt:

"Create a 30-day posting calendar for a Shorts channel about [NICHE].

Include:

Best time according to time zone [YOUR ZONE]

Optimal frequency

Mix of formats per week"

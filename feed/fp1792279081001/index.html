STOP BUYING TECH THAT LOOKS GOOD. BUY TECH THAT EARNS ITS SPACE. ⚡

Published 2026-10-17

A gadget is only worth it when it makes your day easier. That is the standard. 👇

Before you buy your next device, ask three questions:

1️⃣ DOES IT SOLVE A REAL PROBLEM?
A power bank should keep you moving. Earbuds should make calls and focus easier. A smartwatch should give you useful information, not just another screen to charge.

2️⃣ WILL YOU USE IT EVERY WEEK?
If the answer is no, it is probably decoration with a charging cable.

3️⃣ DOES IT FIT THE WAY YOU ACTUALLY LIVE?
Your setup should work for your routine, your budget, your phone, and the places you go. Not somebody else's desk on the internet.

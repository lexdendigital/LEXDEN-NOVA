NOTEBOOKLM + CHATGPT IS ACTUALLY INSANE!!

Published 2026-08-19

NotebookLM turns your sources into research you can actually trust.
Then ChatGPT turns that research into posts, scripts, visuals, content plans, and repeatable workflows.
Here is the full content system 👇🏽

STEP 1: Understand The System
Most creators use ChatGPT like this:
“Give me 10 content ideas.”
That is why the output feels generic.
The better workflow:
NotebookLM = your research brain.
ChatGPT = your content execution engine.
NotebookLM helps you understand the sources.
ChatGPT turns the useful insights into posts, scripts, visuals, calendars, and repeatable systems.
The goal is simple:
Stop creating from random ideas.
Start creating from organized research.

STEP 2: Build One Notebook Around Your Niche
Open NotebookLM and create one notebook for the topic you want to become known for.
Upload useful sources:
• YouTube videos
• PDFs
• reports
• articles
• websites
• customer questions
• competitor content
• your own notes
• research papers
• audio files
Do not upload random information.
Choose sources your audience would actually care about.
Then ask:
“Analyze these sources and identify:
1. the biggest problems people are trying to solve
2. the questions that appear repeatedly
3. the most surprising insights
4. common mistakes
5. useful frameworks
6. strong opinions or debates
7. topics people misunderstand
8. practical actions someone could take today
Organize the findings into clear sections.
Focus on useful content opportunities, not generic summaries.”

STEP 3: Turn The Research Into Content Pillars
Once NotebookLM understands the sources, do not ask for random post ideas yet.
First, build the map.
Use this prompt inside NotebookLM:
“Turn these sources into 5 clear content pillars.
For each pillar, give me:
1. the main audience problem
2. why people care about it
3. the most useful insights from the sources
4. beginner questions
5. advanced questions
6. common mistakes
7. myths worth correcting
8. practical examples
9. 10 content angles
10. the sources supporting the pillar
Make the pillars different enough that the content does not feel repetitive.”
You can also generate a Mind Map to see how the ideas connect.
This gives you structure before you create anything.

STEP 4: Create A Research Brief You Can Reuse
Now ask NotebookLM to turn the - into one clean document.
Use this:
“Create a content research brief from these sources.
Include:
1. target audience problems
2. recurring questions
3. strongest insights
4. useful statistics or facts
5. surprising findings
6. myths to challenge
7. examples or case studies
8. content pillars
9. 30 possible topics
10. source references for important claims
Keep the brief clear and practical.
Write it so another content strategist could use it without reading every source.”
Save the response as a note.
This becomes the source material for ChatGPT.

STEP 5: Create A ChatGPT Project For Your Brand
Open ChatGPT and create a Project for the page, business, or client.
Add:
• the NotebookLM research brief
• your audience description
• your tone of voice
• your best previous posts
• your content rules
• examples you like
• topics to avoid
• your posting frequency
• your main goal
Then add this instruction:
“You are my content strategist.
Use the uploaded research and brand files as the foundation for every answer.
Do not generate generic ideas.
Prioritize:
1. specific audience problems
2. useful outcomes
3. strong hooks
4. simple language
5. fresh angles
6. accurate claims
7. content people would save or share
When an idea is weak, say so clearly and replace it with a stronger one.”
Now ChatGPT is not starting from zero every time.

STEP 6: Build A Content Bank From The Research
Inside the ChatGPT Project, use this:
“Use the uploaded research brief to create a content bank.
Give me:
1. 30 text-post ideas
2. 20 Reel ideas
3. 10 carousel ideas
4. 10 visual infographic ideas
5. 10 opinion posts
6. 10 mistakes-to-avoid posts
7. 10 step-by-step guides
8. 10 beginner-friendly ideas
9. 10 advanced ideas
10. 10 sequel ideas based on the strongest topics
For every idea, include:
• the hook
• the audience problem
• the useful takeaway
• the best format
• the CTA
• the content pillar
Rank the top 20 ideas by potential.
Avoid repeating the same angle with slightly different words.”
This gives you a real content library instead of one random calendar.

STEP 7: Turn One Idea Into A Complete Content Package
Once you choose an idea, do not ask for only one caption.
Use this:
“Turn this idea into a complete content package:
[INSERT IDEA]
Create:
1. 10 hook options
2. the strongest hook and why it works
3. the full Facebook text post
4. a comment thread with real value
5. a 45-second Reel script
6. a carousel slide structure
7. a visual-generation prompt
8. a short version for X
9. a LinkedIn version
10. 3 sequel ideas
11. one pinned comment
12. one CTA
Rules:
• keep the language simple
• avoid empty hype
• avoid robotic phrasing
• make the value clear quickly
• keep claims accurate
• make every version feel native to the platform”
One researched idea can become an entire content campaign.

STEP 8: Use ChatGPT Images For The Visuals
You do not need to open another tool for every visual idea.
Ask ChatGPT to turn the post into a clear visual concept.
Use this:
“Create 5 visual concepts for this post:
[PASTE POST]
For each concept, give me:
1. the main visual idea
2. why it would stop the scroll
3. the subject
4. the background
5. the composition
6. the emotional expression
7. the text overlay, if needed
8. the exact image-generation prompt
Keep the designs simple.
Use one clear focal point.
Avoid crowded layouts, tiny text, and generic AI visuals.
Rank the concepts from strongest to weakest.”
Then generate the strongest concept and refine it.

STEP 9: Turn Winners Into A Repeatable System
The best creators do not start from zero every day.
After publishing, paste your results into ChatGPT:
“Analyze these posts:
[PASTE POSTS WITH REACH, LIKES, COMMENTS, SHARES, SAVES, AND CLICKS]
Tell me:
1. which topics worked best
2. which hooks worked best
3. which formats worked best
4. which posts generated the most shares
5. which posts generated the most useful engagement
6. what patterns appear across the winners
7. what I should stop posting
8. what I should double down on
9. 20 new ideas based on the strongest patterns
10. 5 experiments for next week
Do not only rank the posts.
Explain why the winners worked.”
Then send the strongest topics back into NotebookLM and research them more deeply.
That is how the system improves every week.

STEP 10: Add Optional Automation
Once the manual system works, make it easier to repeat.
Inside ChatGPT, create a recurring Task:
“Every Sunday, remind me to review my content performance.
Ask me to paste the weekly numbers.
Then help me identify the winners, the weak posts, and the 10 strongest ideas for the next week.”
You can also use agent mode for multi-step work when it is available on your plan.
Examples:
• researching a topic across websites
• organizing files
• collecting useful information
• helping prepare a report
• completing a longer online task with your approval
Do not automate a weak workflow.
Build the system manually first.
Automate only after you know what works.

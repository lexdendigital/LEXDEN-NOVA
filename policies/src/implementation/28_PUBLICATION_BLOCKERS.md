# LEXDEN NOVA — PUBLICATION BLOCKERS & READINESS GATES

These are the remaining items that must be resolved before the public legal pages are treated as final contractual/privacy documents.

## BLOCKER 1 — Operator identity

Replace:

- `[LEXDEN LEGAL ENTITY NAME]`
- `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`
- `[LEGAL NOTICE EMAIL]`
- `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`

Do not invent these values.

## BLOCKER 2 — Marketplace/payment contract model

Document the exact relationship between NOVA, independent sellers and the payment provider.

Confirm whether ordinary NOVA checkout will use Paystack split/subaccount settlement or another approved architecture.

## BLOCKER 3 — External checkout classification

Decide which products are genuinely external-access/referral transactions and ensure their UX clearly says payment occurs outside NOVA.

## BLOCKER 4 — Production privacy inventory

Audit the production build for every cookie, local-storage key, analytics tag, SDK, Firebase component, Cloudinary/Brevo integration and other data flow.

The final Cookies Notice must match reality.

## BLOCKER 5 — Youth feature matrix

Define a simple feature matrix:

- public/free browsing;
- account creation;
- paid buying;
- selling;
- affiliate participation;
- withdrawals;
- messaging;
- content posting;
- AI actions.

Then document the age/guardian rule for each feature.

## BLOCKER 6 — Privacy governance

LEXDEN currently has no dedicated legal/privacy team. Assign an operational owner for privacy requests, security incidents and regulator communications. Formal DPO/registration requirements should be assessed against NOVA's actual processing scale and status.

## BLOCKER 7 — Tax/legal registrations

Confirm applicable business, tax, invoicing, VAT, withholding and cross-border obligations for LEXDEN and participating Creators. This pack intentionally does not invent a tax treatment.

## BLOCKER 8 — Prohibited-product/provider compatibility

The platform's final product categories must be checked against Paystack's current acceptable-use/ineligible-business rules and any future payment provider.

## BLOCKER 9 — ADR route

Choose the actual ADR provider/process NOVA will use, if any, rather than publishing a placeholder reference to an unnamed mediation body.

## BLOCKER 10 — Final legal review

Before NOVA becomes a large paid marketplace, obtain external Nigerian legal/privacy review when resources allow. This is especially important for the operator/consumer/creator contracting model, payment structure, minors, data protection, international buyers, tax and liability wording.

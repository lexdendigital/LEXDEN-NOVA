# LEXDEN NOVA — INTERNAL PERSONAL-DATA BREACH RESPONSE PROCEDURE

**Status:** Operational baseline  
**Owner:** LEXDEN NOVA operations/security lead

## 1. Trigger

Use this procedure for suspected or confirmed unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, alteration or destruction of personal data.

## 2. First response

1. Record the incident time and reporter.
2. Contain affected credentials/sessions/storage where possible.
3. Preserve logs and evidence.
4. Identify affected systems/data categories.
5. Prevent evidence destruction during containment.

## 3. Assess

Determine:

- what data was involved;
- how many people may be affected;
- whether sensitive/high-risk data was involved;
- whether unauthorized access actually occurred or is only suspected;
- whether third-party providers are involved;
- whether the event affects payment information or credentials.

## 4. Notification

Follow the notification requirements of applicable law and current regulator guidance. Do not insert an invented 72-hour rule unless the applicable legal instrument actually requires it for the incident in question.

For Nigeria, assess the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and current NDPC requirements/guidance.

## 5. User communication

Where notification is legally required or materially helpful to protect users, communicate plainly what happened, what is being done and what users should do.

## 6. Post-incident

Document root cause, containment, remediation, policy/control changes and lessons learned.

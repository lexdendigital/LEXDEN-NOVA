# LEXDEN NOVA — INTERNAL MODERATION, TRUST & RISK ENFORCEMENT POLICY

**Status:** Operational baseline

## 1. Risk philosophy

NOVA uses a risk-based model to protect buyers, Creators and the platform.

Risk signals are signals, not automatically criminal findings.

## 2. Suggested levels

`INFO` → `LOW` → `MEDIUM` → `HIGH` → `CRITICAL`

Suggested actions:

`MONITOR` → `REVIEW` → `HOLD` → `SUSPEND` → `ESCALATE`

## 3. Important signals

- off-platform payment solicitation;
- suspicious payment links;
- fake verification claims;
- account/identity mismatch;
- repeated refund/dispute patterns;
- malware/file anomalies;
- impossible or misleading delivery coverage;
- sudden high-risk product changes;
- affiliate self-dealing;
- repeated failed fulfilment;
- serious consumer-safety complaints;
- unusual payout patterns;
- unauthorized AI actions.

## 4. Human oversight

Material suspensions, creator bans, major payout holds and ambiguous high-impact cases should have human review unless emergency containment is required.

## 5. Evidence

Every material enforcement action should record:

- target entity;
- triggering signals;
- evidence references;
- rule/policy invoked;
- action taken;
- actor/system identity;
- timestamp;
- expiry/review date where applicable.

## 6. False positives

Automated risk systems should have a correction/appeal path. One weak signal should not be presented to the subject as conclusive proof of wrongdoing.

# LEXDEN NOVA — INTERNAL REFUND & DISPUTE OPERATIONS PROCEDURE

**Status:** Operational baseline

## 1. Intake

Create a dispute record tied to the order and, where relevant, the product version and policy snapshot.

## 2. Classify

Suggested categories:

- non-delivery;
- late delivery;
- defective/unsafe goods;
- not as described;
- digital access failure;
- missing/corrupt file;
- service non-performance;
- programme cancellation;
- external checkout complaint;
- fraud/payment dispute.

## 3. Evidence

Use the least amount of personal data necessary to resolve the case. Relevant evidence can include payment reference, delivery/tracking events, signed/OTP proof, buyer confirmation, digital access events, creator messages and listing snapshots.

## 4. Holds

A material dispute may trigger a temporary payout hold or listing pause according to risk rules.

## 5. Decision

Decision records should state the facts, applicable policy, evidence reviewed, outcome and any appeal route.

## 6. Refund execution

Refund only from authoritative server/provider state. Record provider reference, amount, currency, status and timestamp.

## 7. Reconciliation

Refunds and chargebacks must flow into the creator ledger and affiliate commission logic so historical balances are not silently corrupted.

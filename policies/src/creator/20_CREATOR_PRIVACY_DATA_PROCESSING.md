# LEXDEN NOVA CREATOR — PRIVACY & DATA-PROCESSING PROVISIONS

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. Creator data

Creator onboarding and operation may require processing of identity, contact, public profile, business, payout and fulfillment information.

## 2. Public vs private

The Creator agrees that information intentionally supplied for a public profile (for example display name, category, profile image and public social links) may be displayed publicly.

Private KYC, bank-account, date-of-birth and confidential delivery information must not be displayed publicly except where law requires or the Creator explicitly and lawfully chooses a public field.

## 3. Buyer data handled by Creators

A Creator may receive buyer information necessary for fulfilment/support. The Creator must:

- use it only for the relevant transaction/service and lawful support;
- protect it against unauthorized access;
- not sell or repurpose it without an appropriate legal basis;
- not publish it in reviews, social posts or product examples without lawful permission.

## 4. Return / deletion

Creators must follow NOVA retention and deletion directions where appropriate. Legal or dispute records may need to be kept longer.

## 5. Incident reporting

Creators must promptly report known or suspected unauthorized disclosure of buyer data to NOVA.

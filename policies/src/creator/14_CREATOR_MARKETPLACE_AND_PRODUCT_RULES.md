# LEXDEN NOVA CREATOR — MARKETPLACE & PRODUCT RULES

**Version:** 1.0-draft  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

## Mandatory hierarchy

NOVA mandatory rules + applicable law + the approved product policy = the effective rules for a Creator transaction.

A Creator policy cannot waive a non-waivable legal right or contradict a mandatory NOVA safety rule.

## Required listing integrity

Creators must accurately identify the offering type, price, inclusions, exclusions, prerequisites, delivery/access method, material limitations and support route.

## Product approval

Products must be approved before full deployment where NOVA's review process requires it. A product may remain unavailable while evidence is requested.

## High-risk changes

Changing a product from digital to external access, replacing a paid file, changing the external URL, changing fulfillment, changing material claims, changing price significantly, changing refund terms or changing delivery coverage may trigger re-review.

## Prohibited creator conduct

Do not:

- evade review;
- upload unlawful/infringing material;
- impersonate a verified business;
- misstate identity or ownership;
- request NOVA buyers to pay through another route for a NOVA transaction;
- publish unsafe or malicious files;
- manipulate reviews or affiliate attribution;
- hide material fees/restrictions;
- misstate delivery coverage;
- make unsupported health, income or performance guarantees.

## Buyer safety

Creators must disclose material safety, compatibility, age, delivery, environmental or usage information relevant to the product.

## AI use

Creator AI tools must operate inside NOVA's authentication, permissions, risk, policy and audit framework.

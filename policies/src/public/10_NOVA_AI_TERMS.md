# LEXDEN NOVA — AI ASSISTANT TERMS

**Version:** 1.0-draft  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. What NOVA AI is

NOVA AI is an assistant embedded in LEXDEN NOVA. It can help users understand products, analyze information, draft content, generate ideas, monitor permitted workflows and perform other features the platform explicitly enables.

## 2. User responsibility

AI output may be incomplete, wrong, outdated or unsuitable. Users should review output before relying on it, especially for legal, financial, health, security, compliance or other high-stakes decisions.

## 3. User-approved actions

Where NOVA AI can act on behalf of the user, the action should be based on a user request and the approval flow presented by NOVA.

The fact that a user approved an instruction does not convert every possible AI mistake into the user's fault. NOVA remains responsible for obligations that cannot lawfully be disclaimed, including where the issue arises from NOVA's own unauthorized execution, security failure, negligence or other actionable conduct.

## 4. Unauthorized AI action

If an AI action occurs that the user did not request and approve, the user should immediately contact NOVA Customer Care at lexdendigital@gmail.com and provide available evidence such as timestamps, screenshots, order IDs or activity records.

NOVA will investigate, contain the issue where possible and correct or reverse actions where it is technically and legally possible to do so.

## 5. Policy and risk controls

AI should operate inside the same authentication, authorization, marketplace-rule, risk and audit controls that apply to the relevant human workflow.

AI does not override platform rules, payment-provider rules, legal requirements or administrator controls.

## 6. No professional advice

NOVA AI is not a lawyer, accountant, doctor, financial adviser, regulator or payment-provider representative. Users should obtain appropriate professional advice where a decision materially depends on specialized legal or professional judgment.

## 7. Privacy

AI interactions may involve personal data and are handled according to the Privacy Notice and applicable provider arrangements.

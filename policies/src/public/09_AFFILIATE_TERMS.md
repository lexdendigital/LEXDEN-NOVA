# LEXDEN NOVA — AFFILIATE PROGRAM TERMS

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. Eligibility

Affiliates must provide accurate account information and satisfy NOVA's applicable eligibility and verification rules.

## 2. Attribution

Qualifying commissions are calculated from NOVA's authoritative transaction and attribution records. Historical commission records should retain the applicable commission-rate snapshot.

## 3. No self-dealing

Do not earn commissions through self-purchases, fake accounts, fabricated traffic, cookie/referral manipulation, collusion or other attribution abuse.

## 4. Marketing claims

Affiliate promotions must be truthful and consistent with the underlying offer. Do not invent income claims, health claims, guarantees or features that the seller has not substantiated.

## 5. Disclosure

Where a commercial relationship or commission must be disclosed, the disclosure should be clear and close to the relevant recommendation or promotion.

## 6. Spam and privacy

Do not spam, scrape private data, use deceptive messages or process personal information without an appropriate legal basis.

## 7. Commission reversals

A commission may be reversed when the underlying transaction is refunded, cancelled, charged back, fraudulent, self-generated or otherwise becomes ineligible.

## 8. Fees and withdrawals

Affiliate withdrawals may be subject to verification, reserve controls and the applicable platform/withdrawal fee shown to the Affiliate before the relevant transaction or request.

The current affiliate withdrawal fee is **{{AFFILIATE_WITHDRAWAL_FEE_PCT}}%** of the gross amount requested. The exact fee and the amount you will receive are shown before you submit a withdrawal request.

NOVA may change fee rates prospectively when permitted, with historical records retaining the rate applicable to earlier qualifying transactions.

## 9. Suspension

NOVA may restrict, hold or suspend affiliate activity when abuse, fraud, attribution manipulation or material policy violations are suspected.

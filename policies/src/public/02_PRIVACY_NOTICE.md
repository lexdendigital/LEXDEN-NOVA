Where processing depends on consent, you may withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawfully completed before withdrawal, subject to legal limitations.

## 5. Children and younger users

NOVA is designed to provide access to useful resources, including free/public resources, while applying additional safeguards to higher-risk features.

Some features may be restricted to adults or require parental/guardian involvement because they involve payment, selling, payouts, contracts, verification or other material obligations.

Where the law requires parental/guardian consent, age assurance or additional safeguards for a child's personal data, NOVA will apply those requirements. Free access does not automatically make privacy rules disappear.

NOVA does not intentionally create a system allowing minors to bypass a legal age or consent requirement by falsely representing their age.

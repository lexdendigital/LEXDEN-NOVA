# LEXDEN NOVA — YOUTH ACCESS & MINOR SAFETY POLICY

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]  
**Support:** lexdendigital@gmail.com

## 1. Our goal

LEXDEN wants useful knowledge, tools and opportunities to remain accessible to young people. NOVA therefore distinguishes between **general/free access** and **higher-risk commercial features**.

## 2. Free and public resources

Free/public resources may be accessible to younger users where permitted by applicable law. That does not mean NOVA can ignore privacy, safety, consent or platform rules simply because no money changes hands.

## 3. Accounts and age-sensitive features

Different features may use different age gates. Creator selling, affiliate withdrawals, payout setup and other financial/contractual features are **18+ by default** unless NOVA launches a legally and operationally supported guardian-managed programme.

## 4. No age-bypass system

NOVA will not intentionally build a workflow that asks a child to misrepresent their age in order to obtain access to a feature legally restricted to adults.

Where age assurance is required, NOVA may request age/date-of-birth information or another proportionate safeguard.

## 5. Parent/guardian involvement

Where applicable law requires parent/guardian consent or involvement, NOVA will use an appropriate process rather than relying on an unverified checkbox alone.

## 6. Safety

Young users should never be asked to publicly expose private contact details, precise location, financial information or identity documents merely to access ordinary free resources.

Creators must not target children with deceptive, exploitative or unsafe offers.

## 7. Reporting

A parent, guardian or young user can report a safety concern to lexdendigital@gmail.com. NOVA may restrict access while investigating serious safety concerns.

## 8. Changes

Because child-privacy requirements can differ by jurisdiction and can change, NOVA may tighten feature-level age requirements where needed.

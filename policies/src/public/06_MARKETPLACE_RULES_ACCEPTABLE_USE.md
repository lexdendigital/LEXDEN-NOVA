# LEXDEN NOVA — MARKETPLACE RULES & ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. Purpose

These rules protect buyers, Creators, Affiliates and LEXDEN. They apply to marketplace listings, accounts, comments, reviews, digital files, services, external links and promotional activity.

## 2. Prohibited or restricted products

NOVA may prohibit or restrict products/services including, without limitation:

- illegal goods or services;
- stolen or counterfeit goods;
- products infringing copyright, trademark, publicity, privacy or other rights;
- weapons, ammunition and regulated weapon accessories;
- illegal drugs, controlled substances and prohibited supplements;
- explosives, destructive devices and dangerous chemicals;
- pornography or prohibited sexually oriented goods/services;
- gambling/lottery activities without required legal and provider approvals;
- unlicensed financial, lending, investment, remittance, cryptocurrency or foreign-exchange activities;
- identity documents, private financial records or personal data sold as products;
- malware, credential stealers, destructive code, unauthorized remote-access tools or similar malicious software;
- deceptive get-rich-quick, pyramid, Ponzi or similar schemes;
- products that Paystack or another payment provider will not support;
- other products prohibited by Nigerian law or the law applicable to the transaction.

The final prohibited-category list may be expanded as NOVA's providers, product categories and legal obligations evolve.

## 3. Deceptive conduct

Do not:

- publish false or unsubstantiated claims;
- manipulate reviews;
- impersonate another person or business;
- conceal material fees or restrictions;
- misrepresent delivery coverage or availability;
- use a fake "verified" status;
- fabricate testimonials, earnings claims or before/after claims;
- hide external payment links inside images, text, QR codes or product metadata.

## 4. Off-platform circumvention

A Creator may communicate legitimate support information, but may not redirect a transaction initiated on NOVA to an unapproved external payment route to evade NOVA's checkout, buyer protection, platform fees, records or safety controls.

## 5. Malware and file safety

NOVA may quarantine, scan, reject or remove uploaded files. Security checks reduce risk but are not a guarantee that every malicious file will be detected.

## 6. Reviews and community content

No spam, review-buying, fake engagement, harassment, doxxing, private data disclosure or unlawful content.

## 7. Enforcement

NOVA may issue a correction request, warning, visibility restriction, listing pause, payout hold, account suspension, termination or permanent exclusion.

Serious cases can receive immediate action where delay creates material risk.

## 8. Appeals

Where an appeal route is offered, the affected user may provide relevant evidence. NOVA may protect confidential security methods where disclosure would make the platform easier to abuse.

# LEXDEN NOVA — ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]  
**Contact:** lexdendigital@gmail.com

LEXDEN NOVA aims to make its marketplace and important account/support functions usable by as many people as reasonably possible.

We aim to improve accessibility through readable text, logical structure, keyboard-accessible controls where practical, meaningful labels, usable focus states, responsive layouts and alternatives for important information.

Accessibility is an ongoing improvement process. Third-party content or external websites linked from NOVA may not be under NOVA's control.

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, email lexdendigital@gmail.com with the page/feature and a description of the problem. Screenshots or device/browser details are helpful when available.

## 1. How to complain

Use the relevant product/order support flow where available or email lexdendigital@gmail.com.

Include:

- your name/account email;
- order or product reference;
- seller/Creator name if known;
- the problem;
- relevant dates;
- what resolution you are requesting;
- supporting evidence where available.

## 2. What happens next

NOVA may:

1. acknowledge the complaint;
2. determine whether the issue concerns NOVA, an independent seller or an external provider;
3. request information from the relevant parties;
4. review transaction, delivery/access and policy records;
5. propose a resolution or escalate the issue.

## 3. Urgent matters

Fraud, suspected account takeover, unsafe products, malware, serious privacy concerns and other immediate-risk matters should be reported as soon as possible.

## 4. Consumer redress

NOVA's internal process does not remove the right to use an available regulator, payment-provider dispute mechanism, ADR route or court where applicable.

The FCCPA 2018 emphasizes understandable consumer information, full price disclosure, cancellation/return rights in qualifying circumstances and complaint/redress mechanisms. NOVA's policies are designed around those principles.

## 5. ADR

Where suitable, NOVA may use negotiation, mediation or another lawful ADR mechanism before court proceedings.

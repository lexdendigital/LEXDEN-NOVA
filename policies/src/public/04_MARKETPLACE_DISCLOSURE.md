# LEXDEN NOVA — MARKETPLACE & SELLER DISCLOSURE

**Version:** 1.0-draft  
**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

## 1. What NOVA does

LEXDEN NOVA is a LEXDEN marketplace/platform designed to help people discover resources and connect buyers with independent Creators and businesses.

NOVA can contain:

- LEXDEN-owned products;
- Creator-owned products/services;
- external-access products;
- seller-direct/external checkout referrals;
- affiliate-enabled offers.

## 2. Who is the seller?

Every paid marketplace listing should identify the applicable seller/provider.

For an independent Creator, the Creator is responsible for the product/service, fulfilment, accuracy, lawful permissions, customer support and Creator-level policy commitments, subject to NOVA's mandatory policies and applicable law.

## 3. Verified seller badge

A verification badge means NOVA has completed the disclosed identity/business verification step for that seller. It does not mean NOVA guarantees the seller, product or future conduct.

## 4. NOVA checkout vs external checkout

### NOVA checkout

The buyer pays through NOVA's enabled payment flow. NOVA receives authoritative payment information from the approved payment provider and can apply its marketplace refund/dispute/settlement procedures.

### External checkout

The buyer is sent to a seller-controlled or third-party checkout. NOVA does not control that external payment process. The buyer should read the external seller/provider's terms before paying.

A Creator may not convert an ordinary NOVA checkout sale into an unapproved external payment transaction in order to avoid platform controls, records or fees.

## 5. Seller responsibility

Creators and businesses are responsible for:

- lawful products/services;
- accurate descriptions and claims;
- IP permissions;
- fulfilment;
- delivery coverage;
- customer support;
- product-specific refund/delivery terms;
- tax/licensing obligations that apply to them.

## 6. Buyer protection

NOVA can investigate fraud, non-delivery, inaccessible digital access, safety concerns, serious misrepresentation and other material issues. Buyers retain mandatory consumer rights under applicable law.

## 7. Affiliate relationships

Some products may allow approved Affiliates to earn commissions for qualifying referrals. Affiliate content must disclose material commercial relationships when required and must not misrepresent the product.
